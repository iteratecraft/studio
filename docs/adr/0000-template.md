## Context

What forces the decision: the problem, the constraints, and the facts that matter. Cite spec rule ids
and open standards; never cite vendor material (see `CLEAN-ROOM.md`).

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly. One decision per ADR.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what we must now do or check. Name the follow-up tasks.

## Alternatives considered

Each option we did not take, with one line on why.
